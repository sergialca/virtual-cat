## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-sleek, technical, yet intimately responsive environment modeled after elite modern audio mastering workstations and real-time sonic synthesizers. Built for the next-generation voice interface, the visual tone blends high-precision technical performance with organic responsiveness.

The aesthetic philosophy bridges **Dark Studio Minimalism** and **Refined Luminous Glassmorphism**. The environment lives entirely in deep obsidian and low-reflection slate, allowing high-fidelity audio signals, speech transcription waves, and kinetic states to cut through the interface with controlled luminescence.

Key sensory characteristics:
- **Atmospheric Focus**: Visual weight recedes into the background, focusing attention strictly on sound waves, active states, and real-time audio feeds.
- **Controlled Luminescence**: Warm incandescent orange and vibrant coral accents glow through deep dark layers, mimicking illuminated studio consoles, analog peak meters, and laser optics.
- **Physical Precision**: Ultra-thin luminous hair lines, micro-radii, and translucent panels give every interactive element the feel of an engineered, precision-milled physical synthesizer controller.

## Colors

The palette operates on calibrated contrast steps within the sub-visible dark range, illuminated by vibrant thermal accents:

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - `Base Canvas`: `#0d0f12` (Deep Void Obsidian; light-absorbing baseline canvas).
  - `Surface Container 1`: `#14171f` (Dark Obsidian Slate; used for persistent panels and sidebars).
  - `Surface Container 2`: `#1b202c` (Studio Slate; card surfaces, elevated control modules).
  - `Surface Interactive`: `#262c3a` (High-tier hovering layers and input basins).

- **Thermal Accent Energy**:
  - `Primary Accent`: `#ff6b4a` (Vibrant Coral Orange; reserved for active microphone engagement, voice waveforms, and primary callouts).
  - `Secondary Accent`: `#ff7a59` (Luminous Soft Coral; hover states, secondary signal peaks, and interactive ring indicators).
  - `Tertiary Accent`: `#ff5733` (Deep Ember Orange; high-urgency alerts, audio clipping warnings, and pressed actions).

- **Typographic Hierarchy & Luminous Alpha**:
  - `Text High-Contrast`: `#f1f5f9` (Crisp Studio White; optimal 14:1 legibility against base).
  - `Text Muted`: `#94a3b8` (Slate Neutral; telemetry, passive labels, timestamps).
  - `Luminous Border`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` (Micro-edge highlight for layered panels).
  - `Active Glow`: `rgba(255, 107, 74, 0.25)` to `rgba(255, 107, 74, 0.50)` (Ambient photon diffusion).

## Typography

The typographic engine uses a complementary tri-font pairing to establish technical authority, clear narrative readability, and audio equipment telemetry:

- **Headlines (Space Grotesk)**: Chosen for its futuristic, geometric cut and mechanical precision. It mirrors high-end audio hardware panels and studio synthesizers. Tight negative tracking gives headlines structural weight.
- **Narrative & Body (Manrope)**: Delivers clear, warm, and highly legible text for transcriptions, conversational outputs, and complex reasoning blocks. It balances the stark mechanical nature of the headline font.
- **Telemetry & Labels (JetBrains Mono)**: Encodes technical state data, decibel levels, audio sampling rates, latency indicators, and tooltips. Monospaced spacing maintains strict vertical alignment across dynamic numerical values.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a tight 4px baseline sub-grid supporting an 8px modular spacing rhythm. Content conforms to a dynamic responsive fluid grid designed to accommodate low-latency real-time visualizations and conversational threads side by side:

- **Breakpoints & Grids**:
  - `Desktop (≥1200px)`: 12-column fluid grid, `2.5rem` margins, `1.75rem` gutters. Optimized for split studio views (visualizer pane + conversation log + audio mixer console).
  - `Tablet (768px - 1199px)`: 8-column fluid grid, `1.5rem` margins, `1.25rem` gutters. Visualizer docks as a sticky collapsible header; transcription logs claim the central canvas.
  - `Mobile (<768px)`: 4-column fluid grid, `1rem` margins, `0.75rem` gutters. Controls collapse to a thumb-accessible floating dock anchored to the viewport bottom.

- **Component Geometry & Flow**: Gaps between related functional nodes use `space-xs` and `space-sm`. Internal card padding uses `space-md` for compact telemetry and `space-lg` for conversational bubbles. Outer section grouping strictly enforces `space-xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use diffuse drop shadows. Instead, depth is established using **Tonal Layers**, **Dark Glassmorphism**, and **Luminous Accents**:

- **Stacking Tiers**:
  - *Tier 0 (Base Canvas)*: Solid `#0d0f12`. Completely flat, non-reflective background absorption.
  - *Tier 1 (Panels & Shells)*: Semi-translucent `#14171f` layered with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle `1px` top-lit border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
  - *Tier 2 (Floating Cards & Dialogs)*: `#1b202c` at 85% alpha with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`. Border boundary set to `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
  - *Tier 3 (Modals & Overlays)*: `#262c3a` at 90% alpha, surrounded by an ambient shadow tinted in obsidian-black (`0 20px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`).

- **Luminous Edge & Glow Architecture**:
  - Active interactive components project a low-spread radiant halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(255, 107, 74, 0.22), inset 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`).
  - Waveforms, spectrum analyzers, and active indicators emit subtle neon bleed into adjacent dark slate surfaces, making the interface feel alive with sound.

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp technical hardware precision with tactile affordances:

- **Baseline Geometry (0.5rem / 8px)**: Standard form fields, transcription nodes, parameter sliders, audio channel strips, and telemetry tags adhere to the 8px radius.
- **Containers & Glass Panels (`rounded-lg` / 16px)**: Primary conversational card decks, visualizer viewports, and audio setting drawers employ 16px rounding to soften the dark aesthetic.
- **Floating Controls & Action Triggers (`rounded-xl` / 24px and Full Pill)**: Floating audio docks, the master "Talk" trigger, and segmented status chips use exaggerated pills to encourage natural physical contact.
- **Borders**: Strictly limited to ultra-crisp hairline widths (`1px`), avoiding bulky frames.

## Components

- **Primary Action (Talk Button)**:
  - *Resting*: Large circular or pill button surfaced in `#ff6b4a`, text `#0d0f12` in JetBrains Mono semi-bold. Hairline inner stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`.
  - *Listening / Active*: Background switches between `#ff6b4a` and `#ff7a59` with a sustained multi-layered acoustic pulse: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 6px rgba(255, 107, 74, 0.2), 0 0 28px rgba(255, 107, 74, 0.45)`.
  - *Hover*: Transitions to `#ff7a59` with a subtle vertical scale transform (1.02x).

- **Cards & Chat Modules**:
  - Background: `#1b202c` at 85% opacity, backdrop-filter `blur(20px)`.
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
  - Cat AI Response Bubble: Differentiated by an active vertical accent line (`2px solid #ff6b4a`) anchored on the left margin, with high-contrast text (`#f1f5f9`).
  - User Response Bubble: Surfaced in `#14171f`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.

- **Chips & Filter Nodes**:
  - Monospaced labels (JetBrains Mono) housed inside `#14171f` containers.
  - Active state: `#262c3a` background with `#ff6b4a` text and a micro-dot indicator that pulses in primary coral.

- **Form Fields & Inputs**:
  - Dark input basins (`#0d0f12`) with inset borders (`1px solid #262c3a`).
  - Focused state: Border illuminates to `#ff6b4a` with zero-blur ring glow (`0 0 0 1px #ff6b4a`), text typing in crisp `#f1f5f9`.
  - Placeholder: `#94a3b8` at 60% opacity.

- **Checkboxes & Radios**:
  - Custom square-rounded (`4px`) and circular boxes with `#1b202c` fill and `1px solid #262c3a`.
  - Selected state: Solid fill of `#ff6b4a` with crisp `#0d0f12` tick/dot.

- **Audio Waveform & Spectrum Visualizer**:
  - Dynamic SVG / Canvas audio bars rendered with vertical gradients: base in `#ff5733` bleeding upwards into vibrant `#ff7a59` and peaking with bright `#f1f5f9`.
  - Audio sensitivity threshold line rendered in dotted `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.

- **Telemetry & Audio Level Strips**:
  - Vertical decibel ladders with high-speed response. Inactive segments styled in `#1b202c`; active units dynamically fill from `#94a3b8` (safe) to `#ff6b4a` (optimal speech) to `#ff5733` (peak limit).
